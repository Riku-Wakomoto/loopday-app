# Loopday Privacy Policy

> **Draft for legal review.** Items in [square brackets] must be filled in
> before publishing. The Thai version is at [privacy.th](privacy.th); if the
> two differ, the Thai version applies to users in Thailand.

Last updated: [DATE]

Loopday is a group video diary: every hour, the members of a small invite-only
group each record a 2-second clip, and the clips of the day play back as one
vlog. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, where it is
stored, and the choices you have.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | Examples | Why we need it | Legal basis (PDPA) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Phone number or Apple/Google sign-in ID, display name | Create your account and show your name to your group | Contract |
| Age | Date of birth, guardian consent for users under 20 | Keep under-13s out and protect minors | Legal obligation, consent |
| Content you create | 2-second clips (video and sound), captions, messages, reactions, selfie reactions | Show them to the members of your group | Contract |
| Groups | Groups you create or join, invite codes | Run your groups | Contract |
| Settings | Language, country, quiet hours | Send hourly prompts at the right times | Contract |
| Device | Push notification token, device model, OS version, app version | Send hourly prompts, fix bugs | Contract, legitimate interest |
| Safety | Reports you make or receive, blocks, access logs (IP address, time) | Handle abuse and meet legal duties to keep logs | Legal obligation, legitimate interest |
| Usage | Which features are used, crash reports | Improve the app | Legitimate interest |

We do **not** collect your precise location, your contacts, or face
recognition data, and we do not sell your personal data. Clips are not public:
only the members of the group a clip was recorded for can see it.

The camera and microphone are used only while you are recording. If you do
not allow the microphone, clips are recorded without sound.

## 3. Who can see your data

- **Your group.** Members of a group see your display name, clips, captions,
  messages and reactions in that group. They do not see your date of birth or
  phone number.
- **Exports.** Any member can export the day's vlog as a video file and share
  it outside Loopday. Please only share clips of people who agree.
- **Our service providers**, who process data on our behalf under contract:
  Supabase (database and sign-in, Singapore), Cloudflare (video storage and
  delivery, R2), Expo (push notifications), Sentry (crash reports),
  PostHog (usage statistics), Apple and Google (sign-in, notifications and app
  stores).
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it, such as a court order in
  Thailand.

## 4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Singapore (Supabase) and on Cloudflare's network, and
is accessed by us from Japan. Thailand's PDPA allows these transfers when
appropriate safeguards are in place. We rely on [standard contractual clauses
with each provider / your consent]. Japan has data protection law
(the Act on the Protection of Personal Information) that applies to us.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Clips and their captions | 30 days after the day they were recorded, then deleted |
| Messages and reactions | Until you delete them, leave the group, or delete your account |
| Account and profile | Until you delete your account |
| Access logs | 90 days, as required by Thailand's Computer Crime Act |
| Reports | 1 year after the report is closed |
| Crash and usage data | 90 days |

Deleted clips are removed from storage within 30 days, and from backups
within a further 30 days.

## 6. Children and teenagers

Loopday is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their
data. If we learn that a user is under 13, we delete the account.

Users under 20 in Thailand (under 18 in other countries) must confirm that a
parent or guardian agrees to their use of Loopday. A parent or guardian can
withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us, and we will delete the
account. We do not show personalised ads to minors.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it,
restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service, and you
can withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your clips and your account
in the app (**Settings → Delete account**). For other requests, email
**[SUPPORT EMAIL]**. We reply within 30 days.

If you are in Thailand, you can also complain to the Personal Data Protection
Committee (PDPC).

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access inside our company is
limited to staff who need it, for example to review a report. Clips are not
end-to-end encrypted, because we may need to review reported clips.

## 9. Changes

We will tell you in the app before an important change takes effect.
